# Changelog

## v0.1.2 — Initial public publication snapshot (2026-08-22)

- Public identity standardized as **ALFADEL**.
- Two editorial workflows: standardization and preservation of historical forms.
- Preserve mode distinguishes already-historical forms from standard forms that have licensed historical alternatives.
- Standard forms with historical alternatives receive a distinct visual state and enter the editorial review/navigation queue.
- Already-historical forms are preserved without normalization review.
- Improved handling of Arabic clitics, attached pronouns, weak-final forms, hamza-seat alternation, and morphology-sensitive historical normalization.
- Added `.txt` and `.docx` import and `.txt` / `.tsv` export workflows.
- Added local browser interface and analysis-progress feedback.
- Public distribution excludes copyrighted lexical resources and the controlled corpus; only zero-byte placeholders are retained.
