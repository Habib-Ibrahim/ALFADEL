# Provenance and excluded resources

## ALFADEL

ALFADEL is the software application distributed in this repository. Its name refers to **ʿAbd Allāh ibn al-Faḍl (عبد الله ابن الفضل)**, whose scholarly activity included the revision and correction of Arabic religious texts.

## Dictionaries and lexica

The lexical resources used during ALFADEL's development and testing were developed within the **AAW project**. These dictionaries and lexica are copyright **Don Davide Righi**.

They are **not distributed with ALFADEL**. No rights to copy, redistribute, or relicense those lexical resources are granted by this repository. The files under `resources/lexicon/` and `resources/orthography/` are zero-byte structural placeholders only and contain no lexical data.

## Controlled corpus

The controlled corpus used during development derives from the **e-Cheikho project**, developed between **CEDRAC** and **GREgORI**.

The corpus is **not distributed with ALFADEL**. The file under `resources/controlled_corpus/` is a zero-byte structural placeholder only and contains no corpus data.

## Reviewed overlays

The public release leaves `resources/reviewed_overlay/` empty. This avoids redistributing lexical material or additions derived from restricted resources. The software supports such local overlays, but populated files must not be committed to the public repository unless their redistribution rights are independently established.

## Screenshots

The screenshots under `docs/screenshots/` document the ALFADEL interface and editorial workflow. They do not reproduce the excluded dictionaries or controlled corpus as standalone resources.
